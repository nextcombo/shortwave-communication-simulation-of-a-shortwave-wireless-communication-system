1. Frequency Characteristics
Short-wave communication can cover a relatively long distance due to its longer wavelength, which allows it to propagate through the ionosphere via reflection and refraction. This unique advantage makes short-wave communication particularly effective in long-distance communications.
2. System Composition
Transmitter: It modulates audio, data, and other signals onto the short-wave carrier and amplifies them before sending them through an antenna. The transmitter typically includes an audio processing unit, modulation unit, and power amplifier.
Antenna: It is the device for transmitting and receiving electromagnetic waves. Short-wave antennas come in various forms such as dipole antennas, monopole antennas, etc., and design considerations include frequency, directionality, and gain.
Receiver: It receives signals from the transmitter, demodulates and amplifies them to recover the original information. The receiver typically includes a tuning circuit, demodulation unit, and audio output.
3. Modulation Methods
Common modulation methods used in short-wave communication include:
Amplitude Modulation (AM): The amplitude of the signal varies with the audio signal, simple and easy to use, but sensitive to noise.
Frequency Modulation (FM): The frequency of the signal varies with the audio signal, strong in interference resistance, but requires a large bandwidth.
Single Sideband Modulation (SSB): It eliminates both the carrier and one sideband, saving bandwidth, suitable for long-distance communication.
4. Transmission Characteristics
Short-wave signals have different transmission characteristics under different weather conditions and time periods. Typically, during the night, the reflection effect of the ionosphere is better, allowing signals to travel further.
5. Application Scenarios
Amateur Radio: Enthusiasts communicate, experiment, and participate in competitions.
Maritime and Aviation Communications: Ships and airplanes use short-wave for long-distance communication.
Emergency Communications: In special circumstances such as disasters, short-wave communication can serve as an important backup communication method.
6. Advantages and Disadvantages
Advantages:
Long-distance communication capabilities.
Relatively simple and cost-effective equipment.
Disadvantages:
Greatly affected by weather and terrain.
Limited bandwidth, resulting in lower transmission rates.
Summary
Short-wave wireless communication systems still hold an important position in long-distance communications due to their unique propagation characteristics and relatively simple technical requirements, especially playing an irreplaceable role in emergency and special occasions.
